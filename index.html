Teaching and Learning

T&L toolkit

Clear explanation

Use precise vocabulary, worked examples and visual models to explain abstract concepts such as memory, CPU cycles, networks and algorithms.

Checking understanding

Use hinge questions, mini-whiteboards, code tracing and short diagnostic quizzes before moving to independent practice.

Feedback that moves learning on

Focus feedback on accuracy, logic, decomposition, vocabulary and the quality of explanation.

High Performance Learning

HPL in Computer Science

Advanced cognitive performance

  • Meta-thinking: explain how a solution works.
  • Linking: connect hardware, software and networks.
  • Analysing: trace algorithms and evaluate efficiency.

Values, attitudes and attributes

  • Agility: debug and improve after feedback.
  • Hard working: practise until code is reliable.
  • Empathy: design safe, inclusive digital solutions.
Responsible AI support

AI learning prompts

Use AI as a coach, not a shortcut. Students should ask for explanation, feedback and next steps, then improve their own work.

Explain

“Explain this Computer Science concept at my level, then give me one worked example and one quick check question.”

Improve

“Read my answer and tell me one strength, one misconception, and one sentence I should rewrite using better vocabulary.”

Practise

“Create five retrieval questions on this topic. Do not give the answers until I have tried first.”

Academic honesty reminder: AI can help you understand and improve, but assessed work must be your own thinking and your own final response.

Review and retrieval

Assessment and revision tools

Retrieval quiz

Low-stakes questions for vocabulary, concepts and key procedures.

  1. What is an algorithm?
  2. What does selection mean in programming?
  3. Name one input device and one output device.
  4. What is the purpose of encryption?

Exam response builder

Supports structured answers using command words such as describe, explain, compare and evaluate.

Progress reflection

Students identify confidence, misconceptions and a precise next step after each unit.
